# Descending-final path clearance

Before retaining a runway final, the pilot predicts up to120 fixed ticks (four seconds) of uninterrupted final guidance. The prediction uses the same600m centreline lookahead, terrain-relative height profile, bounded bank actuator, vertical acceleration and conserved total airspeed as the live controller. Each predicted physical fragment is swept against the existing conservative4m hull terrain/solid query. Contact or malformed world source releases that aircraft's reservation and selects its existing physical circuit. The forecast stops at the existing400m downstream go-around boundary.

The NASM routine reads only the caller's current body/sidecar and owned selected facility plus shared terrain/solids. It writes no body, route, fuel, lease or facility; the route caller performs its own abort/release. Work is bounded by120 ticks, constant stack scratch and fixed authored solid capacity, with no actor scan or allocation. Six SysV nonvolatile GPRs and stack are preserved. Public record sizes and AIR_RETURN stay unchanged; approach policy3 and its explicit120-tick horizon join independent canonical compatibility hashes. UDP45, schema0xfbb518e4, content0xd01a1822 require compatible rebuilt peers.

Native evidence in `docs/evidence/air-final-clearance-native.log` includes42 caller/source/ABI/read-only cases, all12 clear role/side/front configurations and four paired physical obstacle flights, repeated twice. A baseline real clear flight places an initial-only solid below the initial horizontal trajectory but inside the future descent. The original current-velocity warning is clear. Candidate rejects the final and clears its lease; omitted-only-preview control admits it. During the first20 actual ticks candidates stay in circuit and roll/turn laterally more; the controls retain final intent until ordinary world avoidance intervenes. Both retain HP50,8 bombs/180 cannon stores and5/7m step distance, with bounded physical bank/vertical changes. No live body/fuel/health/stores/clock renewal is used. All repeated traces match. Both may later recover onto a newly clear final; a lower120-tick minimum height is not a safety failure by itself. Tests retain the existing world warning/contact in both branches.

Initial new observers incorrectly assumed no final recapture anywhere in120 ticks, then compared whole-run/early height and an arbitrary1m lateral delta. Actual safe recapture and terrain-relative cruise show those assumptions were wrong. Final observer measures immediate admission/lease rejection, actual first20 circuit intent and bounded physical lateral turn, with paired omitted-preview response; failed observer logs are retained. Production preview behavior did not change for these observer corrections.

This is a finite-horizon prediction of one uninterrupted approach controller, not a guarantee about future externally interrupted motion, dynamic destruction, physical aircraft collisions, wreck cover, ground runway occupancy or emergency/fairness scheduling. Existing authority world/boundary/separation/defense decisions still apply. Landing, energy/stalls/variable speed, guarded touchdown/rollout, finite depot-backed service and takeoff remain unfinished. Native microtiming is diagnostic only; unchanged scale and client/co-op gates establish their own scopes. Full game acceptance and owner-approved code licensing remain open.
