# Aircraft turn load envelope

The production NASM `air_bank_step` now limits commanded coordinated turns by available lift and role load policy. Authority and the read-only120-tick final-path preview call this same function; no alternate cosmetic turn controller was added.

For speed `v` in metres per30Hz tick, level stall reference `vs`, and role limit `nr`, the available coordinated load is `n=min(nr,0.9*(v/vs)^2)`. The permitted turn tangent is `sqrt(n*n-1)*0.999999`; requested yaw times speed is clamped to gravity times that tangent before the existing `atan2` bank conversion. Existing heading gain, yaw bounds, angular bounds and roll-rate limits still apply. The numerical guard conservatively absorbs float angle rounding; the existing independent angle/yaw tolerances remain unchanged.

Bomber normal/emergency limits are3g/4.5g with a2.2m/tick (66m/s) level stall reference. Fighter limits are6g/8g with2.3m/tick (69m/s) reference. These are authored tactical-aircraft parameters, not claims of an exact real aircraft model. The maximum maneuver reserve is used by explicit map-boundary steering and an acquired unavailable-facility holding orbit. A finite real defensive commitment, a physically acquired fighter intercept and a finite valid own remembered bomber mission may also use maneuver reserve. Ordinary waypoint flight, world-warning turns without a qualifying mission and unobstructed supplied-base finals use the normal envelope. This preserves tactical evasions and fighter protection while bounding peak load; it does not read hidden live enemy coordinates. Holding reserve selection separately checks active approach absence, public return mode and current private holding generation/phase; it does not change the boundary flag or cancel approach height. A fighter at5m/tick has at most approximately4.2533g available with the reserve, so slowdown automatically reduces the commanded bank even during emergency demand.

Roll remains continuous. An initially supplied over-limit bank, or transition from emergency to normal, unloads over bounded roll steps rather than snapping. The function validates original role/emergency/error/speed/bank domains before any calculations. Current speed domain remains5..7; the stall references describe available lift, but no low-speed domain, stall dynamics, lift-induced drag, kinetic/potential energy integration, safe ground contact or landing is enabled.
