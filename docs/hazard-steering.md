# Incoming-hazard goal steering

`hazard_choose_goal(EDI entity, XMM0 impactX, XMM1 impactZ, XMM2 radius)` returns EAX 0 and goal XZ in XMM0/1, with EDX 1 for dispersion or 2 for shelter. EAX -1 / EDX 0 means no valid local goal. It reads entity and terrain authority, preserves the SysV nonvolatile registers, allocates 96 bytes of stack scratch, and writes no entity, health, events or persistent state. The integration owner controls observations, response duration and ordinary-speed movement.

Only living ground kinds 0–2 with health inside their actual 100/400/160 HP limits and finite clear start positions are accepted. Center coordinates must be finite in 0–8000 and radius finite, positive and at most 512 m. An actor already beyond radius + 12 m receives no goal. Radial escape attempts stop at radius + 12 m or 36 m of travel, whichever is nearer. Co-located actors use stable ID modulo 8 for compass dispersion. A blocked radial route tries ±45° and ±90° tangents, requiring increased distance from the blast center and an exact clear straight path. Invalid or unreachable choices return no goal rather than crossing a wall or teleporting.

Before dispersion, the helper considers four faces of at most 16 actual terrain obstacle records. Each candidate lies 2 m outside a face, clamping its other coordinate to the actor's nearest face point. It requires at most 24 m travel, `terrain_path_clear`, and an occluded `terrain_los` from center terrain height + 1 m to goal terrain height + 2 m. The closest valid candidate wins. This is physical terrain occlusion. It does not claim that every wall blocks every blast policy or that a farther wall makes a safe multi-corner escape.

`tests/hazard_steering_probe.asm` is a development-only return-value and ABI wrapper. Link it alongside `tests/terrain_probe.asm` and the normal simulation modules, then run `python3 tests/test_hazard_steering.py LIBRARY`.

Seed 923 passed eight distinct exact-center directions, a 36 m capped response, physically executed terrain movement at actual role steps 0.12/0.5/0.2 m per tick, a wall-blocked radial tangent, three reachable corner shelters checked against real height and LOS, invalid coordinates/radii/IDs/health/air actors, blocked starts and no-goal edge cases. Checks confirmed unchanged entity bytes and simulation checksum, plus nonvolatile-register preservation on valid and invalid calls. An independent double-precision segment/rectangle oracle checked 195 valid randomized routes around all five actual terrain solids; 24 of these additionally ran movement to the goal at role speeds. The existing terrain suite also passed in the worker checkout.

These helper tests do not prove world hazard awareness, sustained artillery survival, audiovisual quality or army-scale costs. Those require integrated scenarios and profiling. The search has bounded obstacle/face and radial/tangent candidates; it does not plan a multi-corner route or guarantee shelter in open terrain.
