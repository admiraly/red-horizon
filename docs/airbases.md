# Authored airbases and recovery destinations

Six terrain-conforming runways, each 1,200 × 80 metres, occupy X=2,000/6,000 and Z=2,000/4,000/6,000. `content/terrain/airbases.json` generates shared NASM descriptors and GLSL material geometry through `tools/airbases.py`; builds reject stale generated output. No terrain height is flattened. The production terrain material draws asphalt, edge lines, centre dashes and threshold bars. Tracked AI and player vehicles receive paved traction only when their whole conservative body circle fits a runway or existing road capsule.

Recovery prefers the aircraft's original side/front facility. A facility must currently belong to that side, be a command/depot role, connected, alive and uncontested with valid flags. Otherwise the aircraft selects the nearest eligible runway, including captured facilities; exact ties choose the lower ID. Ownership is checked before reading facility condition. Queries read neither enemy actors nor facility inventories. If none is available, existing home holding remains a withdrawal fallback, with no fabricated service or renewed fuel.

The facility association uses the existing strategic site network. It does not establish physical access roads, aprons or taxi routes between sites and runways. Aircraft continue the existing airborne recovery/holding policy. There is no approach/deceleration, touchdown, landing gear, rollout, taxiing, runway traffic arbitration, ground servicing, refuel, repair or rearm. Aircraft still have existing fixed role airspeeds; genuine fuel exhaustion still causes powerless glide and terrain casualties. The entire game specification remains incomplete.

`air_base_goal(EDI own physical aircraft ID)` returns a base ID or −1; successful XMM0/1 contain centre XZ, failure preserves fallback XMM0/1. All SysV nonvolatile GPRs, R8/R9 and stack are preserved. `air_runway_body(XMM0 X, XMM1 Z, XMM2 radius)` returns 1 contained / 0 outside / −1 invalid. Inputs are finite map coordinates and finite nonnegative radius up to 8,000 metres. Descriptors are six read-only 32-byte records: four f32 centre/extent fields and four u32 site/birth-side/front/reserved fields. Public entity/aircraft and wire record sizes remain unchanged. Ground surface policy is version2 and recovery policy version3; protocol42, schema `0x192a9d67`, content `0x9037b3f4` require rebuilt compatible peers.

Native evidence (`docs/evidence/airbases-native.log`) covers 48 facility cases, invalid/ownership/read-only/ABI controls, 384 exact Fraction-based circle boundary cases, full strip bounds excluding authored ridge/relief breaks and five static obstacles, and 18 actual low-clearance swept paths. Maximum sampled grade is 0.005585. This proves current static terrain clearance, not landing contact, dynamic actors, wrecks or traffic safety. Two paired 1,800-tick public fighter flights, with one initial home-site destruction per side, reach within 5.274 metres of an alternative centre; the omitted-facility control stays 973/1,310 metres away. Health, ammunition and role speed remain unchanged, without mid-flight renewal.

Production NASM-embedded fragment tests pass on software GL and native Arc A770: 54 independent samples, 108 draws each, all six strips, painted markings, asphalt and unchanged outside pixels against an omitted-material control. Diagnostic textures/flat geometry are declared; HDR-specific colour acceptance is not separately established. The actual 8,192-unit native client screenshot (`evidence/airbases-client-native.png`) shows a runway on genuine battlefield terrain. Its development observer (`evidence/airbases-capture-observer.py`) pauses simulation after settling, changes only input/camera presentation and verifies five equal authoritative hashes and read-only final census. It establishes presentation, not live flight, performance, art acceptance or a dense visible battle.

`airbases-traction.log` contains actual public AI tank/artillery and player-driven tank runway/off-road comparisons with original motion/health gates. Focused frozen aircraft and network suites passed, preserving baseline/stretched aircraft and real UDP coverage; full extended and fast checkpoint states are recorded in status.md. Historical timeout failures are retained separately.
