# Friendly aircraft convergence avoidance

Friendly aircraft use a fixed-tick, per-side frozen physical snapshot within 500m. At most 25 cells and 64 candidates predict closest relative approach over 60 ticks (two seconds). An approaching trajectory within 30m commits a nonrenewing right-turn avoidance for 24 fighter or 40 bomber ticks, followed by 90/120-tick refractory periods. Existing bank, yaw, speed and vertical limits apply. Boundary and urgent defensive maneuvers retain priority; new combat is suppressed during avoidance. Enemy poses cannot consume the friendly candidate budget. Private 16-byte generation/timer state participates in authority hashing; derived indices are rebuilt. UDP40 and public layouts remain unchanged; policy content is 0xb8a03929.

Independent 1500-view oracle (254 warnings), invalid/ABI/readonly/lifecycle/budget tests pass. These narrow encounters establish improvement, not a universal safe-distance guarantee.
